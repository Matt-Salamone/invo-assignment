# 06: Reduce oversized lesson context with traceable derivatives

**What to build:** When lesson context is oversized or excessively extraneous, packet preparation creates usable summaries or trimmed materials while preserving lesson-critical information and traceability.

**Blocked by:** 05: Issue and manage one self-contained lesson packet.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The planning agent assesses size and relevance for the target chatbot, documents its basis and uncertainty, and selects summaries or trimmed derivatives when appropriate without assuming exact chatbot limits.
- [ ] Routine packet summaries and trimming proceed under workflow authorization. New intake conversions still follow the earlier conversion-permission requirement.
- [ ] Originals remain unchanged. Derivatives and issued packets record source identity, page or slide references, included content, derivation provenance, and material limitations.
- [ ] Preparation verifies that reduced context retains lesson-critical details. Relevant omissions are disclosed rather than silently dropped.
- [ ] Issuance preserves the actual derivative files used by the lesson chatbot, so later regeneration cannot silently change the issued snapshot.
- [ ] Synthetic public-operation checks use source pages with identifiable content to verify retention, source references, original preservation, and omission reporting. Selected-page PDF checks inspect actual page content or rendering where practical rather than filenames alone.
