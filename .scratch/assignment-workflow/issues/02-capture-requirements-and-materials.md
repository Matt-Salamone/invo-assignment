# 02: Capture assignment requirements and prepare approved materials

**What to build:** The planning agent turns supplied assignment instructions and course materials into a durable intake record, clarifies consequential uncertainties, and makes approved materials usable without losing their origins.

**Blocked by:** 01: Install the skill and initialize or resume an assignment.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Intake records required deliverables, constraints, grading criteria, source inventory, consequential uncertainties, and stated assumptions. Routine decisions do not cause unnecessary clarification.
- [ ] Deadline and preferred lesson length are optional pacing inputs; unavailable answers do not prevent intake.
- [ ] Before a new intake conversion runs, the agent explains its tools, resulting artifacts, and relevant fidelity limits and obtains actual permission. Declined, unavailable, and unreadable materials remain explicit gaps.
- [ ] Originals are preserved. Converted derivatives retain provenance and source references sufficient to trace consequential content.
- [ ] Supplied course materials govern the assignment context; external sources are distinguished and missing context is exposed rather than invented.
- [ ] Skill instructions and public workspace operations persist intake for a new planning conversation. Synthetic behavioral checks cover conversion decisions and provenance, including refusal to record a conversion as approved without the required decision record; instruction dry runs complement checks that cannot prove actual consent.
