# Adapt teaching and hand control back

After importing a report, inspect its actual questions, learner responses, assistance and gaps. Explain the evidence separately from your recommendation. Coverage, assisted explanations and independent explanations have different implications; none proves retention or correctness. Recommend revisiting gaps, extra practice or targeted follow-ups when needed, and reduce redundant teaching when actual responses support it.

Apply routine changes automatically using `teaching-adapt` with JSON containing `kind: "routine"`, `lesson_id`, relevant `import_id`, `reason`, and an `action` such as extra practice or a narrower explanation. This appends a durable adaptation without expanding plan scope or minutes. Keep pacing manageable relative to the student's deadline and preferred duration. Read adaptations when preparing the next requested packet.

For a substantial scope or workload change use `teaching-adapt` with `kind: "substantial"`, `reason`, and `plan` containing the complete proposed `scope`, `pacing`, and `lessons` (the same fields as plan-propose). Explain the before/after workload and ask for the student's actual decision. `pending_teaching` holds the proposal without changing the active plan. Run `teaching-decide` with its `proposal_id`, `decision: "approved"` or `"declined"`, and the actual `decision_record`. Approval creates the next plan version, preserving prior versions and decisions. Finish or explicitly abandon the outstanding packet before approval. Changed lesson objectives reset progress; review relevance of older evidence before using it again.

When the student overrides a progression recommendation use `progression-override` with `lesson_id`, `choice: "continue"` or `"revisit"`, and the actual `decision_record`. This records a separate plan-version-specific student choice; it leaves evidence, gaps and progress labels unchanged. Explain limitations honestly while respecting the student's choice.

`final-handoff` with `mode: "complete"` checks that every objective has independent observed evidence without gaps and a continue recommendation, or an explicit continue override for that lesson. Resolve pending proposals and packets first. If teaching is incomplete but the student chooses to end it, use `mode: "early"` with the actual `decision_record`. This preserves and abandons any outstanding packet. Either operation records the final status and prompt: **Create your own original version of the assignment.** Present that prompt without prescribing another process; later assistance is the student's choice.

A fresh planning chat reads `status` to recover the active plan, evidence, adaptations, `pending_teaching`, `teaching_decisions`, `progression_overrides`, and `final_handoff`. Do not infer a decision from elapsed time or relabel missing evidence as understanding.
